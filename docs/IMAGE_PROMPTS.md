# AI-generated recipe photography

These project-bound raster assets were generated with the built-in Codex image generation tool. They are AI-generated illustrations of the listed recipes, not photographs of meals prepared from the recipes.

The final web assets are `public/images/*-photo-v1.jpg` (209–372 kB per image). PNG originals are preserved in `assets/recipe-image-sources/`. JPEG derivatives retain the original composition and dimensions; only web encoding was changed. The app loads the JPEGs and labels them as AI-generated.

The whole starter bank is covered. Additional exact prompt sets: [chicken and salmon](IMAGE_PROMPTS_CHICKEN_FISH.md), [classics](IMAGE_PROMPTS_CLASSICS.md), [vegetarian recipes](IMAGE_PROMPTS_VEGETARIAN.md).

## Krämig tomatpasta med linser

Asset: `assets/recipe-image-sources/tomatpasta-photo-v1.png`

Prompt: Create a photorealistic, natural food photograph for a Scandinavian everyday dinner app. Close-up of homemade creamy red tomato sauce with red lentils and short pasta (rigatoni or fusilli), served in a shallow simple ceramic bowl. Ingredients should visibly match the recipe: pasta, creamy tomato and red lentil sauce, modest finely grated hard cheese made with vegetarian rennet, a few fresh basil leaves; no chicken, no meat, no spinach, no unrelated ingredients. Contemporary approachable home cooking, appealing natural window daylight, light oak or off-white kitchen surface, warm but fresh color, professional realistic food texture, home-cooked rather than restaurant-styled. 3:2 landscape composition, entire bowl/plate comfortably fits centered in frame with some breathing room for responsive cropping. No text, no hands, no watermark.

Serve with a visible small portion of cottage cheese (keso) and lingonberry jam, the only toppings/accompaniments. Contemporary approachable home kitchen, natural daylight, pale oak or off-white surface, fresh appetizing greens and berry red, realistic home-cooked texture, not restaurant-styled. 3:2 landscape composition, entire plate fits centered in frame with breathing room for responsive cropping. No unrelated food, no extra garnish, no text, no hands, no watermark.
